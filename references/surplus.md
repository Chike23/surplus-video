# Surplus: what every video is selling

Every video ends on Surplus, after the story. Claim only what is written here. When the owner tells you something new about the
app (a feature, a price, a link), add it here so later videos can use it.

## The app
- **Name:** Surplus (say it "SUR-plus"; on screen always "Surplus", capital S).
- **What it is:** an app that helps people **gain weight**: it **tracks calories**, helps them **eat better**, and keeps
  them in a steady calorie surplus so the scale finally moves.
- **Who it is for:** people who struggle to gain weight. Skinny people, "hardgainers", people who "eat a lot but don't
  gain", people with small appetites, lifters who can't bulk, people recovering their weight.
- **The promise, in one line:** "Surplus makes gaining weight a plan, not a guess."

## Features we can name (only these)
- Calorie tracking, to see how much you actually eat. Most skinny people overestimate it.
- A daily surplus target: how much more than maintenance to eat.
- Help eating better: calorie-dense, healthy food choices.

## How Surplus appears in a video (owner's rule)
Never on a phone, a screen or as the app: no phone, no app icon, no app screens or anything that looks like them. It
comes last, after the whole story: as a bridge through the story's character, who acts out the change (eats the
extra meal, the scale finally moves, a proud pose), or as a plain plug in a new scene when no bridge is good enough
(`references/script.md`, "Surplus, last"). The plug says only what Surplus is for, never how it works, along the
lines of "If you want to gain weight or bulk, Surplus is a bulking app that helps you do it." Only the name and the CTA may be lettered in the
style (the easel card, a marquee).

## Calls to action (the user picks one per video; a plug ending always uses 3, "Comment BULK and I'll send you the app")
1. **Link in bio:** spoken "Download Surplus, link in bio." On screen: "Surplus · link in bio".
2. **App Store search:** spoken "Search Surplus on the App Store." On screen: "Search **Surplus** on the App Store".
3. **Comment keyword:** spoken "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send you Surplus." On screen: "Comment **KEYWORD**". The post
   caption adds "(follow first so my DM reaches you)". The user chooses the keyword (one word, caps, e.g. BULK, GAIN).

## Tone
Friendly, direct, a coach who has been skinny too. Second person ("you"). Confident with no hype words ("insane",
"game-changer", "hack"). Honest about evidence. Never body-shaming, never "fix your body". It's about getting
stronger, healthier, filling out.

## Health guard rails
- Explain, don't prescribe. Use ranges and "most people" language. Typical sensible gain is about 0.25–0.5 kg
  (0.5–1 lb) a week from a surplus of about 300–500 kcal a day.
- Any number on screen or in the voice needs a source in plan.md (study, guideline, or well-established figure).
- No medical claims (curing, treating). If a topic touches eating disorders or a medical condition, keep it general
  and suggest a professional.
